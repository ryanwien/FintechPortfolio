Generated by `python -m fraud.run` (seed 7, runtime 40s). Every number below comes from that run.

## Data

- 331,302 synthetic transactions from 1,800 customers; 5,075 fraud (1.53%).
- Fraud by type: card_testing 1,813, ato 1,361, high_value 822, merchant_compromise 657, friendly_fraud 422.
- Time split (30-day months): warm-up [0, 1] (feature history only), train [2, 3, 4] (106,485 txns), validation [5] (37,642), test [6, 7] (79,313), shifted month 8 (41,915).
- Label delay: median 10.9 days, p90 33.1 days. At the training cut-off 15.7% of training-window fraud had not been reported yet, so the model trained on it as 'good'.

## Cost-sensitive decisioning

Costs: missed fraud = full amount; review = $3.00 analyst time, stops 90% of the fraud it sees, $1.00 friction for a good customer; false decline = 3.0% lost margin + $10.00 customer friction. Review capacity <= 1% of traffic. Bands are chosen on the validation month and applied unchanged to the test months.

| Policy | Review if score in | Decline if score >= | Reviews | Declines | False declines | Fraud $ stopped | Expected loss (test) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| hgb | [0.01788, 0.05849) | 0.05849 | 756 (0.95%) | 1,388 | 373 | 95.6% | $55,448 |
| logreg | [0.06227, 0.106) | 0.106 | 709 (0.89%) | 1,696 | 790 | 93.3% | $82,070 |
| rules | [1, 1) | 1 | 0 (0.00%) | 3,313 | 2,531 | 90.0% | $155,768 |
| approve all | - | - | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0% | $815,306 |

**HGB cuts expected loss by $100,320 vs the rules baseline over 2 test months (64.4% lower, $50,160/month)**, and by $26,622 vs logistic regression.

HGB loss breakdown (test): fraud_missed $38,950, false_decline $13,513, review_ops $2,985.

Same frozen bands on the shifted month: HGB expected loss $39,926, rules $129,684; fraud dollars approved $20,749 of $178,252.
